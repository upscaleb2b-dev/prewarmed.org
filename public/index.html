Alpha Foundation — Nonprofit & Community Impact

PreWarmed is a commercial service that exists to fund charitable work. It sells email infrastructure to businesses, and all of its net proceeds go to Alpha Foundation, a charitable organization in Kasur, Pakistan.

How PreWarmed and Alpha Foundation are connected

They are two separate things with one purpose. PreWarmed is the business: it sells prewarmed inboxes, domains and deliverability services at commercial prices. Alpha Foundation is the charity: it carries out the charitable work that PreWarmed's proceeds pay for.

PreWarmed is owned by Alpha Forge. Orders made through prewarmed.org are fulfilled by PreWarmed.

Where the money goes

  1. You place an order through prewarmed.org. Payment is taken and the order is fulfilled.
  2. The direct costs of your order are paid. That covers things like Google and Microsoft licences, domain registrations and the work of setting up and warming your inboxes.
  3. Everything left goes to Alpha Foundation. The foundation uses it for its charitable work in Kasur.

To ask how Alpha Foundation has used the funds it received, or to request its financial records, email founder@alpha-f.org.

Alpha Foundation's work

Alpha Foundation's mission, programs and updates are published on its own website, alpha-f.org. That is the authoritative source for what the foundation does.

Buying from PreWarmed is not a donation

When you order from PreWarmed you are paying a commercial price for a commercial service. Your payment is not a charitable gift, you will not receive a donation receipt, and it does not carry any tax benefit. The proceeds that reach Alpha Foundation are given by PreWarmed, not by you.

If you would like to donate to Alpha Foundation yourself, contact the foundation through alpha-f.org or at founder@alpha-f.org.
