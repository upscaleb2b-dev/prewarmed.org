Who we areNo mystery

We own the infrastructure. That is the whole point.

WarmInboxes is our infrastructure arm. Prewarmed and fresh Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and Azure inboxes, with domains and DNS configured before you log in. We sell it, and we are not going to be coy about that on our own research site.

It is also the reason this data exists. Nobody publishes placement cohorts, decay curves or burn causes in this market because almost nobody operates enough infrastructure to measure them. We do, so we measure them, and then we publish the numbers next to everyone else's on the same terms.

If the data ever stops backing us, you will read it here first. That is the deal, and it is what makes the rest of the site worth reading.

How this site works

Named, and reviewed

Every page carries an author and a reviewer who did not write it. For research, the reviewer re-runs the analysis from the committed script before sign-off.

Dated and re-verified

Published date, last-verified date and the verification interval sit at the top of every page. A stale page fails our build rather than going quietly out of date.

Corrections in public

Confirmed errors are fixed within five working days and logged with what was wrong and what changed. A research site with no corrections is one nobody is checking.

Free and ungated

No account, no email capture, no gated downloads, no pop-ups. Charts and aggregated data are published under CC BY 4.0 so you can redraw them and check the arithmetic.
