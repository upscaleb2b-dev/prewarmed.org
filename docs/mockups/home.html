How this site works

Named, and reviewed

Every page carries an author and a reviewer who did not write it. For research, the reviewer re-runs the analysis from the committed script before sign-off.

Dated and re-verified

Published date, last-verified date and the verification interval sit at the top of every page. A page past its interval fails our build rather than going quietly stale.

Corrections in public

Confirmed errors are fixed within five working days and logged with what was wrong and what changed. A research site with no corrections is one nobody is checking.

Free and ungated

No account, no email capture, no gated downloads, no pop-ups. Funding, affiliate programmes and rates are published in full on the disclosure page.
